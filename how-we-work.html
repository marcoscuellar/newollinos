01The rules

Three things it will never do.

1

Never invents.

Verifiable sources only. No made-up companies, people or facts. Empty beats plausible.

2

Never softens.

A claim that fails a check is dropped, not hedged and not called “likely”. You see the real signal, unknowns included.

3

Never sends on its own.

ŌLLIN prepares. You review and decide. Nothing sends without your yes.

02Honest labels

Every fact wears its label.

You always know what ŌLLIN checked, what it worked out, and what it couldn’t confirm. A gap stays a gap.

Example data · not real contacts
  • Verified
    Opened a second regional hub in Q3Checked against a source you can open: press release.
  • Inferred

  • Unable to verify
    Routing technology budgetShown as a gap. Never filled with a guess, and never used in a draft.
  • Withheld
    Hiring manager’s emailNot confirmed, so it’s held back. Nothing is sent to an address we’re not sure of.
