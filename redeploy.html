01 / Why it’s hard

Every firm has a process. The cracks are the same.

It starts too late.

Outreach often begins in the last week, or after the last shift. By then, good contractors are already talking to other firms.

Their profile is from day one.

A year on a project brings new skills and systems. The record still shows what they could do when you hired them.

Everyone thinks someone else has it.

The account manager assumes the recruiter is on it. The recruiter assumes the account manager is. No one calls.

It starts from job boards.

When the next order lands, the search goes outside, not to the people already on your bench.
